# Project Nova Q3 2026 Financial and Operations Report

## Executive Summary
Project Nova delivered record performance across all operating segments in the third quarter of 2026. The distributed computing infrastructure network processed 450 million cryptographic micro-transactions with zero network downtime across twelve geographic clusters.

## Key Financial Metrics
Revenue for the quarter reached $14.2 million, representing an increase of 42 percent compared to the prior quarter. Total operating expenses were contained at $8.6 million, leading to an operating margin of 39.4 percent. The treasury reserve holds 125000 GEN tokens valued conservatively at market parity.

## Infrastructure and Security
The core protocol upgrade, code-named Falcon-7, was deployed successfully on September 15, 2026. Independent security audits were completed by OpenSec Labs with zero critical vulnerabilities discovered. Average transaction latency decreased from 185 milliseconds to 48 milliseconds following the optimization of the consensus pipelining engine.

## Personnel and Expansion
The engineering division onboarded 14 new full-time distributed systems engineers across Berlin, Tokyo, and San Francisco, bringing total headcount to 84 employees worldwide.
